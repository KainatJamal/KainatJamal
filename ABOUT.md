# About me

</div>

I came into Computer Engineering wanting to understand how things work underneath the frameworks — not just how to call an API, but what happens on the wire when I do. That instinct runs through everything on this profile.

Most of my early projects are what you'd expect from a full-stack track: React front ends, Node/Express APIs, Firebase and PostgreSQL on the backend, JWT auth, real-time features with Socket.IO. I built those deliberately, across four internships, to get comfortable shipping complete products rather than isolated exercises.

The project that changed how I think about engineering was my final year project, **PakRescue AI**. I needed to stream live video and send drive commands to a WiFi-connected rescue robot, and the manufacturer's companion app was the only reference — closed source, undocumented, and never built for anyone else to extend. So I opened it up: decompiled the APK with JADX, captured its traffic in Wireshark, and reverse-engineered the TCP control protocol and the RTP/JPEG video stream byte by byte until I understood it well enough to rebuild it natively in Kotlin. That taught me more about networking, Android internals, and disciplined debugging than any course did — it's the project I lead with now.

I graduate from SSUET in 2026, and I'm looking for a team where I can keep working at that intersection: solid full-stack fundamentals, applied to problems that don't come with a clean spec.
